# Career direction reference

Approved direction · August 31, 2026

## Background

Mohit Garg is an applied power architect at NVIDIA with about five years of experience and a Master's in ECE from UCLA. His work builds deep-learning-based features that allocate runtime power budgets across CPU, GPU, memory, and I/O to optimize performance per watt for a given workload.

He wants a faster-paced, well-funded startup environment and a new problem domain that still takes advantage of his hardware and machine learning background. An interesting hardware-aligned role remains welcome.

## Positioning statement

> I’ve spent five years at NVIDIA building deep-learning-based runtime power allocation across CPU, GPU, memory, and I/O. I’m looking to apply that hardware-aware ML and optimization experience to inference infrastructure or onboard robotic systems.

## Approved direction

You want a new problem domain where your existing expertise gives you an advantage—not necessarily another power-architecture job.

### 1. Robotics inference and onboard ML systems

**Most interesting intersection of your background and a genuinely new challenge.**

Work on getting perception, planning, and learned policies to execute efficiently on a robot’s compute platform.

- **You bring:** heterogeneous-system understanding, resource tradeoffs, runtime optimization, and ML experience.
- **You gain:** robotics, real-time inference, model deployment, and physical-world constraints.
- **Watch for:** roles that genuinely own ML execution and performance, rather than primarily device drivers or platform maintenance.

This combines the two directions you liked. You wouldn’t have to choose between robotics and efficient AI systems immediately.

### 2. Inference runtime and performance engineering

**Strongest choice if you want to become substantially more software-oriented.**

Work on how models execute: scheduling, batching, memory management, profiling, and adaptive runtime behavior.

- **You bring:** the ability to reason across CPU, GPU, memory, and I/O rather than optimizing one component in isolation.
- **You gain:** inference frameworks, production serving, and potentially kernels or compilers.
- **Watch for:** a role with room to develop those implementation skills, rather than one requiring immediate mastery of an unfamiliar stack.

I’d favor ownership of an inference subsystem over a job that is exclusively benchmarking or reporting performance.

### 3. Hardware/software co-design at an AI accelerator company

**The hardware-aligned option I would absolutely keep open.**

It could be compelling if you help decide how architecture, runtime software, and ML workloads should evolve together.

The key question is whether you would influence architecture and implementation broadly—or mostly repeat your current power-budgeting responsibilities on different silicon. Either could be valuable, but only the former clearly satisfies your desire for novelty.

### 4. ML-driven scheduling and resource management

**Closest transfer of your existing expertise.**

Potentially excellent if the scope expands to workload placement, heterogeneous compute, or cluster-level decisions. I’d rank it below the others only because it could feel more familiar than you want.

### The filter I’d use

For each role, ask:

> What would I be uniquely useful at on day one, and what would I become good at over the next two years?

You want convincing answers to both. As a rough heuristic, look for **60–70% transferable foundations and 30–40% meaningful stretch**, not an exact skill match.

My strongest recommendation now is to search around **robotics inference/onboard compute** and **inference runtime/performance**, with hardware/software co-design as a deliberate third track.

And I’d treat **power optimization as evidence of your ability to optimize complex systems—not as the boundary of your professional identity.**

## Company and role diligence

- Prefer high ownership and short feedback loops; distinguish these from constant emergencies.
- Evaluate funding alongside runway, actual product/customer traction, available compute, and team quality. A funding announcement does not establish financial health.
- Ask what the role owns in its first 90 days and what shipped recently.
- Assess senior individual-contributor roles first. Level depends on scope and impact, not tenure alone.
- Validate hands-on CUDA, compiler, firmware, runtime, production-serving, and robotics requirements against actual experience; do not infer them from NVIDIA employment.
- Location, relocation, remote-work preferences, compensation floor, and the final company watchlist are still undecided.
