## Availability rules

- **Open:** Exact stable ID is present in a valid complete employer feed, or its live employer detail page has the matching identity and application action.
- **Needs recheck:** Timeout, 403/429/5xx, malformed/empty/incomplete feed, unexpected redirect, unrecognized page, or first successful-feed absence. Preserve the record in the active view with its warning.
- **Archived:** Explicit employer closure/not-found message for that exact role, or absence in two successful, nonempty full employer feeds on separate days without an intervening open observation. A plain 404 response alone is not enough; investigate canonical changes or corroborate it. A same-day retry is not a second independent absence.
- **Reopened:** A previously archived stable ID is positively present again. Restore it to active results, clear its current archive date/reason, and retain its availability history and original first-seen date.

Use `applyObservation` in `lib/jobChecking.js` for these transitions, rather than inventing looser rules. Evidence history is limited to the latest 30 daily observations per role, with a permanent current archive date/reason until reopening. New candidates are never automatically added by the deterministic collector; the scheduled agent reviews them first.

## Sources and limitations

- Seven [Ashby public job feeds](https://developers.ashbyhq.com/docs/public-job-posting-api), plus the [Greenhouse public Job Board API](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html) for Anthropic.
- Google uses live public Careers search/detail HTML. Its markup may change; parsing failures are recheck states, not closures.
- Feed presence confirms publication at check time, not a guarantee the hiring team is still actively interviewing. Always confirm before applying.
- Do not paste raw descriptions into the public board; summarize them. The ignored local report is not for publication and may contain irrelevant or unlisted roles.
- Static page status reflects the last published observation. It cannot tell whether a local scheduled task is currently running or whether the Mac is asleep. Scheduling behavior follows the [official scheduled-task documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations?surface=app).
